How I lead

I set strategy clearly enough that the team can make its own calls, then spend my time on feedback and quality rather than approvals.

Coaching and autonomy

Strategy sets the boundaries

I define the design strategy and the framework decisions get made against. Inside it, product designers decide without waiting on me.

Feedback is the job

Most of my week goes into feedback: weekly reviews, structured critique and time on specific work with specific people. I’d rather give a little every week than a lot at once.

I hold the quality bar

I review against a standard the team can apply without me: spacing and layout, design system usage, color and contrast, accessibility.

I lead with every function

I work with product, engineering, insights, compliance and marketing to define what the products are trying to achieve, so design has a say in the roadmap it works from.

AI in the process

My teams build agents for the slow, repetitive parts of the work. One audits our Figma library every month and writes the exact fix for each issue it finds. Another turns a flow into a prototype customers can try while it’s still cheap to change.

Every agent works from a brief and a set of rules, and someone on the team reviews what it produces.

I’m deliberate about what we adopt. A new tool has to fit a real need before the team takes it on, and we drop it once it stops being useful.

How I track design

Design is accountable at three levels at once, and they feed each other. Good craft changes how people behave, and that shows up in the numbers the business already reports. Any surface I own carries all three, so a decision can be traced end to end.

Design quality

Spacing and layout QA, design system component usage, color and contrast, accessibility violations. These keep quality from drifting once a team is moving fast.

It shows up as a design decision

Experience metrics

Completion rate, time to decision, drop-off at each step. At least one has to be perceptual, like effort or confidence, or the layer only measures speed.

It shows up as a behavior change

Business outcome

Conversion, activation, retention. Design is measured on these, the same as the rest of the business.

It shows up as a business result
