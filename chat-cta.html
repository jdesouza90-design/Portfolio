Chat opener · five variants

Motion for the chat button

Each one is the live primary button with a single animation added. They move for one to two seconds and then sit still, so they never pull hard. Each is shown on paper and on the sand panel. The switch at the bottom puts one in the real corner position.

01Shimmer

A soft band of light crosses the ink pill from left to right, then the button sits still. Closest to a shimmering gradient without changing the button's color.

.cta-shimmer · 1.4s sweep every 5s · ::after gradient · background-position

02Trace

A short green comet runs round the outline without stopping. It is the most noticeable of the five because it never rests. Slowing it past 4s makes it calmer.

.cta-trace · 3.6s per lap, continuous · conic-gradient ring · @property angle

03Drift

The fill moves slowly from ink through the greens and back. The lightest point is the site's accent, which is also the hover color, so text contrast stays at 5.9:1 or better.

.cta-drift · 9s, alternating · 300% background-size · background-position

04Ping

A small online dot, and a ring in the accent green that leaves the pill twice, then goes quiet. It reads as someone is here.

.cta-ping · Two rings in 1.7s every 4.5s · box-shadow spread

05Draw

A green outline draws itself round the pill, holds, then fades, while the small sparkle in the icon pops in. Uses the same draw-on move as a signature.

.cta-draw · 1.8s draw, 1.5s hold, every 6s · SVG stroke-dashoffset
