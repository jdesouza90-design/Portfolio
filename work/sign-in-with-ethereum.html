Auth0 · Identity · Web3 login

Sign-in with Ethereum

Sign-in with Ethereum lets a user log in with their Ethereum wallet instead of a username and password, so their identity data stays under their control rather than a provider's.

My roleProduct Design Manager. Led a cross-functional team through two rounds of user testing and delivery.
TeamDesign, engineering and identity product, across Auth0's consumer and developer-facing teams.
ValidationTwo rounds of user testing, fed back into roadmap decisions.
TypeWallet-based authentication for people new to web3.

Summary

Wallet-based login was, at the time, a pattern built for people already familiar with web3. We kept the shape of an ordinary login page instead: one button, a wallet prompt, a signed message, no seed phrases and no new vocabulary, with the profile metadata developers need arriving alongside the session. The design was validated over two rounds of user testing before shipping across Auth0's consumer and developer-facing surfaces. There are no published usage metrics for this project; the outcome below is stated qualitatively.

The problem

Putting users in control

  • AutonomyGiving users complete control over their identity data and how it's used.
  • SafetyProtecting wallet keys with zero-risk architecture and encryption.
  • AdoptionMeeting users where they are with familiar login patterns and seamless flows.

My role

Direction, validation, advocacy

Design direction

Led a cross-functional team, design, engineering, and identity product, through the core tension of making wallet-based identity low-friction for users unfamiliar with web3. Coordinated across Auth0's consumer and developer-facing teams to maintain a consistent experience across the integration surface.

Validation & learning

Led the team through two rounds of user testing, synthesizing findings across designers and feeding conclusions back into roadmap decisions. Managed the testing cadence alongside active delivery, keeping the team aligned and moving.

User advocacy

Directed the design team's approach to education and clarity throughout implementation. Set the principles governing how the team communicated self-custodied identity to mainstream users, and reviewed all design decisions against those principles before handoff to engineering.

The design

A wallet login that reads like any other login

The flow keeps the familiar shape of a login page. One button, a wallet prompt, and a signed message: no seed phrases, no new vocabulary, and the profile metadata developers need arrives with the session.
