Cross-Sell: Vehicle Equity & Home Secured Loans

When an applicant is declined for their primary loan, Cross-Sell asks a few qualifying questions and, where they qualify, presents a secured-loan offer instead of ending the application.

My roleDirector of Product Design. Set the framework, coached two designers, ran weekly reviews.
Team2 designers, with content, product, compliance and engineering across two business lines.
Timeline5 months. Vehicle Equity fully live Oct 30, 2025; Home Secured fully live Jan 29, 2026.
Type0→1 feature inside the lending funnel, across two secured products.

Summary

Best Egg's loan funnel ended at the decline, with no path forward for applicants who didn't qualify for their primary request. We redesigned the decline into a decision point: a short set of qualifying questions, then a secured-loan offer where the applicant qualifies. Vehicle Equity and Home Secured launched five months apart on separate cadences, reusing the same design system components. Combined, the two paths produced $7.56M in new non-affiliate secured-loan originations from launch through February 2026.

The problem

Turning a decline into an opportunity

  • PivotTransitioning from a "hard rejection" to a relevant secondary offer.
  • TrustPreserving brand equity at a high-stakes emotional moment.
  • BusinessMaximizing loan originations from applicants who would otherwise exit the funnel with no recovery path.

My role

Framework, orchestration, coaching

Strategic design

Set the cross-sell design framework across the lending funnel. Defined the pattern for identifying high-intent redirect moments and established consistency across decision points without requiring individual oversight.

Orchestration

Coordinated design, content, product, compliance, and engineering across two secured products running on different release cadences. Managed parallel workstreams, ran weekly design reviews, and maintained alignment across business lines with separate roadmaps.

People development

Used this project as a structured growth opportunity for both designers. Each ran their own stakeholder reviews by the end. My role shifted from directing to coaching as the project progressed.

What I'd do differently

Research timing, sequencing, and what held

Research timing

Qualitative signal was strong going in. A/B test coverage came post-VEL launch. Earlier quantitative testing on the redirect copy would have reduced iteration cycles after go-live and prevented user frustration.

Sequencing

VEL and HSL launched on different cadences because they were scoped by separate GMs, which meant reworking patterns for HSL that were already locked for VEL. Next time I'd push for journey-level scoping before individual product scoping.

What held

The design system investment meant HSL required no component rebuilding. That decision, made months before Cross-Sell was scoped, paid off directly in launch speed.
