Cross-Sell: Vehicle Equity & Home Secured Loans

When an applicant is declined for their primary loan, Cross-Sell asks a few questions and, if they qualify, offers a secured loan instead of ending the application.

Results

Declines that became loans

Best Egg's loan funnel ended at the decline. We turned it into a decision point: a few qualifying questions, then a secured-loan offer for those who qualify. Vehicle Equity and Home Secured launched three months apart, built from the same design system components.

My role

Framework, orchestration, coaching

Strategic design

I set the cross-sell framework for the whole funnel: where a redirect moment is worth offering, and what it looks like every time. The team applied it without me reviewing each decision.

Orchestration

Two products, two roadmaps, two release calendars, with content, product, compliance and engineering on each. I ran the weekly reviews for both so the patterns stayed shared.

People development

The senior product designer and product designer were both running their own reviews with product, compliance and engineering by the end. I started the project directing and finished it coaching.

How we got there

From a dead end to a second offer

The problem

The funnel ended at the decline

An applicant who didn't qualify for their primary loan saw a rejection and a third-party partner offer. There was no path back into Best Egg.

  • PivotThe decline had to become a second offer, not a rejection.
  • TrustApplicants had just been told no, so the offer had to read as help, not a catch.
  • BusinessEvery declined applicant left the funnel with nothing, and so did Best Egg.

The design

From dead end to qualifying flow

The redesigned funnel treats the decline as a decision point: a short set of qualifying questions, then an offer the applicant can accept.

Design decisions

Three decisions inside the flow

Content strategy

The copy moved from "Rejected" to "A different path".

Progressive disclosure

One idea per screen, with the detail behind "Learn more".

Consistent patterns

Built from existing design system components, so the offer looks like the rest of the application.

What I'd do differently

Research timing, sequencing and what held

Research timing. The qualitative signal was strong going in, but the A/B test came after Vehicle Equity launched. Testing the redirect copy earlier would have saved iteration after go-live and spared some applicants the frustration.

Sequencing. Vehicle Equity and Home Secured were scoped by different general managers, so they launched on different calendars and Home Secured had to rework patterns Vehicle Equity had already locked. Next time I'd push to scope the journey before scoping each product.

What held. Home Secured shipped without rebuilding a component. The design system investment behind that was made months before Cross-Sell was scoped, and it paid off in launch speed.
