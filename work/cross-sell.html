Cross-Sell: Vehicle Equity & Home Secured Loans

When an applicant is declined for their primary loan, Cross-Sell asks a few questions and, if they qualify, offers a secured loan instead of ending the application.

My roleDirector of Product Design. Set the framework, coached two designers, ran weekly reviews.
Team2 designers, with content, product, compliance and engineering across two business lines.
Timeline5 months. Vehicle Equity live Oct 30, 2025. Home Secured live Jan 29, 2026.
Type0→1 feature inside the lending funnel, across two secured products.

Summary

Best Egg's loan funnel ended at the decline, with no path forward for applicants who didn't qualify. We turned the decline into a decision point: a few qualifying questions, then a secured-loan offer for those who qualify. Vehicle Equity and Home Secured launched three months apart on separate release calendars, built from the same design system components. Together they produced $7.56M in new non-affiliate secured-loan originations from launch through February 2026.

The problem

Turning a decline into an opportunity

  • PivotThe decline had to become a second offer, not a rejection.
  • TrustApplicants had just been told no, so the offer had to read as help, not a catch.
  • BusinessEvery declined applicant left the funnel with nothing, and so did Best Egg.

My role

Framework, orchestration, coaching

Strategic design

I set the cross-sell framework for the whole funnel: where a redirect moment is worth offering, and what it looks like every time. The team applied it without me reviewing each decision.

Orchestration

Two products, two roadmaps, two release calendars, with content, product, compliance and engineering on each. I ran the weekly reviews for both so the patterns stayed shared.

People development

Both designers ran their own stakeholder reviews by the end. I started the project directing and finished it coaching.

What I'd do differently

Research timing, sequencing and what held

Research timing

The qualitative signal was strong going in, but the A/B test came after Vehicle Equity launched. Testing the redirect copy earlier would have saved iteration after go-live and spared some applicants the frustration.

Sequencing

Vehicle Equity and Home Secured were scoped by different general managers, so they launched on different calendars and Home Secured had to rework patterns Vehicle Equity had already locked. Next time I'd push to scope the journey before scoping each product.

What held

Home Secured shipped without rebuilding a component. The design system investment behind that was made months before Cross-Sell was scoped, and it paid off in launch speed.
