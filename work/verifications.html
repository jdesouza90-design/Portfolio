Best Egg · Consumer lending · Redesign + A/B test

Verifications

Redesigned Best Egg's document verification flow so applicants can see their status after upload. Sized at 7,000 fewer support calls a month and validated in an A/B test before rollout.

My role
Director of Product Design. I set the strategy and directed two designers from discovery through the A/B test.
Team
2 designers, with product, engineering, compliance, operations and the insights team.
Timeline
3 months, from discovery to A/B test delivery.
Outcome
100% upload completion in the A/B test. Sized at 7,000 fewer support calls a month.

Results

Design B resolved the questions in-flow

After uploading documents, applicants had no way to confirm what was pending, accepted or still needed, and a misleading progress bar made it worse. We replaced it with a checklist per document, explicit format guidance and a status that appears the moment a file lands. The opportunity, 7,000 fewer support calls a month, was sized with product and operations before the work began.

How we got there

From a progress bar to a checklist

The problem

Closing the loop after upload

Once a file was sent, the dashboard went quiet. Nothing said whether it was pending, accepted or still needed, so applicants called to ask.

  • Unclear statusAfter submitting, applicants couldn't tell what was pending, accepted or still needed.
  • Ambiguous requirementsThe dashboard didn't say which formats or alternatives were accepted, so applicants called to ask.
  • Progress bar confusionA multi-step progress bar implied steps that didn't exist, right at the point of submission.

The design

One upload per document, one clear status

Design B replaces the progress bar with a document-led checklist: one clear upload per document, explicit format guidance and a status the applicant can see the moment a file lands.

My role

Alignment first, then design

Cross-functional alignment

I got product, engineering, compliance and operations to agree on requirements before design started, and kept the priorities from shifting once it had.

Research integration

With the insights team, we ran A/B usability tests of the current upload flow against the document-led design. The two designers turned the findings into a prioritized list of changes, each tied to a KPI.

Design leadership

I set the design strategy and directed two designers from discovery through the A/B test, with weekly reviews to hold quality across parallel streams.
