Best Egg · Consumer lending · Redesign + A/B test

Verifications

Redesigned Best Egg's document verification flow to give applicants a clear status after upload, reducing support calls and improving conversion. Validated with an A/B test before rollout.

My roleDirector of Product Design. Set the strategy and directed two designers from discovery through A/B test delivery.
Team2 designers, with product, engineering, compliance, operations and the insights team.
Timeline3 months.
TypeRedesign of the document verification flow, validated with A/B usability testing.

Summary

After uploading documents, applicants had no way to confirm what was pending, accepted, or still needed, and a misleading progress bar made it worse. We replaced it with a document-led checklist, explicit format guidance, and a status visible the moment a file lands. The redesign was validated in an A/B test against the existing flow, then rolled out. The opportunity, 7,000 fewer support calls a month, was sized with product and operations before the work began.

The problem

Closing the loop after upload

  • Unclear statusApplicants had no way to confirm what was pending, accepted, or still needed after submitting documents.
  • Ambiguous requirementsMissing specificity on document formats and alternatives drove calls that the dashboard should have resolved.
  • Progress bar confusionA multi-step indicator implied a flow that didn't exist, creating uncertainty at the point of submission.

My role

Alignment first, then design

Cross-functional alignment

Coordinated across product, engineering, compliance, and operations to align on requirements before design work started. Facilitated prioritization that kept the team focused despite competing inputs from stakeholders.

Research integration

Partnered with insights to run A/B usability testing comparing the current upload flow against the new document-led design. Directed two designers on translating findings into prioritized changes tied to KPIs.

Design leadership

Directed two designers through discovery, prototyping, and A/B test delivery across all experiences. Set the design strategy, ran weekly reviews, and maintained quality across parallel work streams.

The design

From ambiguous direction to actionable clarity

Design B replaces the progress bar with a document-led checklist: one clear upload per document, explicit format guidance, and a status the applicant can see the moment a file lands.
