Verifications

Document verification in the Best Egg loan application. Applicants upload three documents and can now see each one’s status instead of calling to ask.

Outcome

68%

Verification rate in the live A/B test, against 58% for control. Sized at 7,000 fewer support calls a month

Role
Director of Product Design
Team
1 senior product designer, 1 product designer
Timeline
3 months
Scope
Discovery to live A/B test

The problem

Once a file was sent, the dashboard went quiet, so applicants called to ask.

  • Unclear statusAfter submitting, applicants couldn’t tell what was pending, accepted or still needed.
  • Ambiguous requirementsThe dashboard didn’t say which formats or alternatives were accepted.
  • Progress bar confusionA multi-step progress bar implied steps that didn’t exist, right at the point of submission.

How I led it

Requirements

I got product, engineering, compliance and operations to agree on requirements before design started, and kept the priorities from shifting once it had.

Usability tests

With the insights team, we ran A/B usability tests of the current upload flow against the document-led design. The senior product designer and product designer turned the findings into a prioritized list of changes, each tied to a KPI.

Direction

I set the design strategy and directed a senior product designer and a product designer from discovery through the A/B test, with weekly reviews to hold quality across parallel streams.

Key decisions

A checklist replaced the progress bar

Design B is a document-led checklist: one clear upload per document, explicit format guidance and a status the applicant can see the moment a file lands.

A clear end to the upload

We saw applicants stall after adding their documents: nothing told them they were done. We added an “I’m done” button that takes them back to the dashboard, and applicants stopped stalling.

Results

The opportunity, 7,000 fewer support calls a month, was sized with product and operations before the work began.
