Refinance offers on native mobile

A refinance offer card in the Best Egg app. Customers who qualify for a lower rate now see it next to the loan it would replace.

Outcome

$1.9M

Results

Originations from a path mobile didn't have

The Best Egg app showed every customer the same invitation to apply for another loan, whatever they qualified for. We replaced it with a refinance offer card on Home and on Offers for customers with a lower rate available. My role

Direction, consistency, coaching

Design direction

I set the design direction and the framework for the card. It leads with the rate, sits beside the loan it would replace and reads as a suggestion, not a promotion.

One offer, every channel

I got product, marketing and compliance to one rule. The offer a customer sees in email, in the web funnel and in the app is the same offer.

Coaching

I split the work between the senior product designer and the product designer: one drew the illustrations, the other designed the experience. I ran the reviews, and each of them took the work through their own product team to ship.

The problem

Mobile showed every customer the same message

The Best Egg app offered every customer the same thing: you're pre-approved for another loan. It understated what eligible customers could get, and the 10% with two active loans got no offer at all. Refinancing earns 1.5 times what a second loan does, and mobile wasn't telling that story.

  • AudienceTwo-loan customers had no offer at all, even when consolidating would lower their cost.
  • StoryA payoff-quote customer with a lower rate available saw a generic invitation instead of the refinance.
  • ParityMobile had to show what web and email showed, or the funnel leaked and incrementality went under-reported.

The design

A refinance card under the loan it replaces

The only thing that changed on the screen is the card. It names the offer, refinance, and the rate the customer is pre-approved for, with the disclosures a tap away. View offer opens the mobile web application, so we shipped in seven weeks without building a native flow.

Design decisions

Four decisions inside the card

  • A suggestion, not a notice

    The copy moved from "Just wanted to let you know" to "Refi might be right for you".

  • The rate, not the amount

    The legacy card led with an amount. This one leads with the annual percentage rate (APR) the customer is pre-approved for, the reason to refinance.

  • An illustration, not a banner

    The lightbulb brings the brand's illustration style, the one in the emails and the web funnel, into the app. The offer looks the same from the campaign to the card.

  • A test, not a guess

    This card was A/B tested against other variants before it shipped. It's the version that won.
