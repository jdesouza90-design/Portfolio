Staking v0.1

Staking v0.1 lets users stake LINK with clear guidance and real-time visibility into pool capacity, reward rate and account status, so they can decide and act without leaving the page.

My roleSenior Product Design Manager. Led design planning and reviews across four senior designers.
TeamFour senior designers in three time zones, with engineering and protocol teams.
TimelineLaunched December 2022 as an early-access release.
Type0→1 staking interface for LINK, from discovery to launch.

Summary

Staking LINK required leaving the interface to check documentation, pool capacity or reward terms elsewhere. We put pool capacity, reward rate and remaining allotment next to the stake action itself, so users could decide and commit without a detour. The v0.1 early-access pool reached 25M LINK staked and hit full capacity within three hours of launch in December 2022.

The problem

Removing friction from the start

  • ClarityGuiding users through staking with intuitive flows and transparent documentation.
  • TrustReal-time visibility into rewards and account status at every step.
  • GrowthBuilding architecture flexible enough to scale with user and feature expansion.

My role

Alignment across design, engineering and protocol

Strategic alignment

Led design planning across a distributed team of four designers spanning three time zones. Partnered with product and engineering leadership to align the team's direction with platform goals, and established the experience principles the team worked from throughout delivery.

Cross-functional leadership

Drove alignment between design, engineering, and protocol teams on decisions with technical dependencies. Facilitated design reviews across teams, resolved conflicting requirements, and represented the design team's position in cross-functional planning.

Design execution

Directed the team through discovery, prototyping, and design validation, reviewing work at every stage, coordinating handoffs between designers, and maintaining quality across a feature set that launched to 25M LINK staked in three hours.

The design

Capacity, rewards and eligibility, visible before you commit

Three steps to early access: connect a wallet, sign a message, view your eligibility. Once in, the pool gauge, reward rate and remaining allotment sit next to the stake action so nobody has to leave the page to decide.
