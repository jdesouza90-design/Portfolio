Staking v0.1

Staking v0.1 lets people stake LINK with pool capacity, reward rate and account status in view. They can decide and act without leaving the page.

Outcome

25M LINK

Staked, with the pool full within 3 hours of launch

Role
Senior Product Design Manager
Team
1 product designer
Time zones
3
Launched
December 2022

The problem

People read the docs in one place, checked the pool in another and took the reward terms on faith.

  • UrgencyStaking hadn’t launched, and the community had no way to participate.
  • Unfamiliar groundThe team had only ever shipped developer tools, never a product built for consumers.
  • RegulationStaking regulation was still unsettled, so the design had to hold up under rules that could still change.

How I led it

Planning

I led design planning with engineering and protocol partners across three time zones, and set the experience principles with product and engineering leadership.

Protocol calls

Where a design decision depended on the protocol, I got design, engineering and protocol into one review and settled it there. I represented design in cross-team planning.

Direction

I directed the work from discovery through validation, reviewed it at every stage and ran the handoffs with engineering. At launch, 25M LINK was staked in three hours.

Key decisions

Everything to decide on one page

Trust in crypto was running low, so the page had to be easy to understand and easy to trust. Three steps to early access: connect a wallet, sign a message, view your eligibility. Once in, the pool gauge, reward rate and remaining allotment sit next to the stake action so nobody has to leave the page to decide.

Screen recording of the v0.1 early-access flow.

The feeds the pool secures

Under the pool sit the data feeds it secures: each feed’s latest answer, its node operators and whether each one responded.
