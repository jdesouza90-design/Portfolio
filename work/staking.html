My role

Design execution

The problem

The community had no way to participate

Staking hadn't launched yet, and the community had no way to participate. It was also Chainlink Labs' first consumer product, after years of developer-only tools, and regulation for staking was still unsettled. People read the docs in one place, checked the pool in another and took the reward terms on faith.

  • UrgencyStaking hadn't launched, and the community had no way to participate.
  • Unfamiliar groundThe team had only ever shipped developer tools, never a product built for consumers.
  • RegulationStaking regulation was still unsettled, so the design had to hold up under rules that could still change.

The design

Capacity and rewards, visible before you commit

Trust in crypto was running low, so the experience had to feel trustworthy and intuitive before anything else. Three steps to early access: connect a wallet, sign a message, view your eligibility. Once in, the pool gauge, reward rate and remaining allotment sit next to the stake action so nobody has to leave the page to decide.
