Staking v0.1

Staking v0.1 lets people stake LINK with pool capacity, reward rate and account status in view, so they can decide and act without leaving the page.

My role
Senior Product Design Manager. I led design planning and reviews across four senior designers.
Team
4 senior designers in 3 time zones, with engineering and protocol teams.
Timeline
Launched December 2022 as an early-access release.
Outcome
25M LINK staked. The pool hit full capacity within 3 hours of launch.

Results

Full capacity within three hours

Staking LINK meant leaving the interface to check documentation, pool capacity or reward terms. We put pool capacity, reward rate and remaining allotment next to the stake action itself, so people could decide and commit without a detour.

How we got there

Everything needed to decide, on one page

The problem

Deciding meant leaving the page

To stake, people read the docs in one place, checked the pool in another and took the reward terms on faith.

  • ClarityThe docs and reward terms lived elsewhere, so the page couldn't explain what people were signing up for.
  • TrustPeople needed to see their rewards and account status at every step, not take them on faith.
  • Growthv0.1 had to be built so later pools and features could land without a redesign.

The design

Capacity and rewards, visible before you commit

Three steps to early access: connect a wallet, sign a message, view your eligibility. Once in, the pool gauge, reward rate and remaining allotment sit next to the stake action so nobody has to leave the page to decide.
