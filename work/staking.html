Staking v0.1

Staking v0.1 lets people stake LINK with pool capacity, reward rate and account status in view, so they can decide and act without leaving the page.

My roleSenior Product Design Manager. Led design planning and reviews across four senior designers.
Team4 senior designers in 3 time zones, with engineering and protocol teams.
TimelineLaunched December 2022 as an early-access release.
Type0→1 staking interface for LINK, from discovery to launch.

Summary

Staking LINK meant leaving the interface to check documentation, pool capacity or reward terms. We put pool capacity, reward rate and remaining allotment next to the stake action itself, so users could decide and commit without a detour. The v0.1 early-access pool reached 25M LINK staked and hit full capacity within three hours of launch in December 2022.

The problem

Deciding meant leaving the page

  • ClarityThe docs and reward terms lived elsewhere, so the page couldn't explain what people were signing up for.
  • TrustPeople needed to see their rewards and account status at every step, not take them on faith.
  • Growthv0.1 had to be built so later pools and features could land without a redesign.

My role

Alignment across design, engineering and protocol

Strategic alignment

I led design planning for four designers across three time zones, and set the experience principles the team worked from with product and engineering leadership.

Cross-functional leadership

Where a design decision depended on the protocol, I got design, engineering and protocol into one review and settled it there. I represented design in cross-team planning.

Design execution

I directed the team from discovery through validation, reviewing at every stage and coordinating handoffs between designers. The result launched to 25M LINK staked in three hours.

The design

Capacity, rewards and eligibility before you commit

Three steps to early access: connect a wallet, sign a message, view your eligibility. Once in, the pool gauge, reward rate and remaining allotment sit next to the stake action so nobody has to leave the page to decide.
