Auth0 · Identity · New capability

No-code tools

No-code tools let teams configure Auth0 login flows, branding and actions visually, without writing code, while leaving the code path in place for developers who want it.

My roleProduct Design Manager. Directed two designers through iterative design and validation.
TeamDesign, product and engineering across multiple product teams, within a global design team of seven.
DeliveryPhased, with organizational buy-in secured up front.
TypeVisual configuration for login flows, branding and actions.

Summary

Implementing Auth0 required writing code, which limited who inside a customer's team could set up or change a login flow. We positioned a no-code capability with Auth0 leadership as a strategic gap, then delivered it in phases: a visual customization panel with a live preview, and a flow builder for assembling, testing and publishing a login journey. The code path stayed available throughout. There are no published usage metrics for this project; the outcome below is stated qualitatively.

The problem

Lowering barriers to implementation

  • AccessLetting users build authentication flows without writing code.
  • ConfidenceHelping new users get started quickly without feeling lost in complexity.
  • ControlPreserving the power and flexibility developers need to customize.

My role

Positioning, alignment, quality bar

Product strategy

Partnered with Auth0 leadership to position no-code tools as a strategic capability gap. Defined the design team's role in making automation accessible to non-technical users and secured organizational buy-in on the phased delivery approach.

Cross-functional leadership

Aligned design, product, and engineering across multiple product teams on a shared experience direction. Managed scope across competing roadmap demands, facilitated prioritization with engineering leads, and coordinated design reviews with stakeholders across the organization.

Design execution

Directed a team of two designers through iterative design and validation of the no-code interface. Set the quality bar, ran structured critique sessions, and ensured output stayed grounded in the behaviors of non-technical users throughout delivery.

The design

Configure the login, build the flow, see it live

Two surfaces: a customization panel where styles, fonts and colors update a live preview of every login screen, and a visual flow builder where a login journey is assembled from actions, tested, and published.

Screen recording of the flow builder: start node, actions, test and publish.
